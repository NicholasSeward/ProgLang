# Week 1 — Discussion

## Your post

Pick a language you know and roast it (or defend it) using Sebesta’s criteria.

Include:

1. **One thing it nails** — which criterion? (readability, writability, reliability, cost, …)
2. **One thing that drives you crazy** — which criterion does it hurt?
3. **The trade-off** — what do you get in exchange for that pain?

Keep it short. Hot takes encouraged. “Python is perfect” with no criterion named does not count.

## Comments

Reply to **at least two** classmates.

Good replies:

- Agree and add another example
- Disagree and name a different criterion
- Ask: would you still use it for *X*?

“Nice post” alone does not count.
